[Payable amount of the prepaid payment instruments]

[Valid period / expiration]
There is no valid period or expiration date.

[Contact information]
Astran Inquiry Desk motitan@astran.jp

[Where the in-app currency can be used]
It can be used within each application.

[Notes on use]

(1) Where the in-app currency you have acquired is currency acquired in exchange for consideration (paid in-app currency), the number of units of paid in-app currency you have acquired is displayed at the time of acquisition (where paid in-app currency and free in-app currency are acquired at the same time, they are displayed separately).

(2) Where you hold both in-app currency acquired in exchange for consideration (paid in-app currency) and in-app currency issued without consideration (free in-app currency), the paid in-app currency is consumed first.

(3) As a general rule, no refund is made after paid in-app currency has been purchased. However, if the Company discontinues the service for its own reasons, the paid in-app currency you have purchased will be refunded to you in accordance with the unit price at issuance. In addition, if you are enrolled in an annual plan, a refund will be made according to the number of months remaining, counted from the month following the month in which the service is terminated (if you are enrolled in a monthly plan, no refund is made according to the number of days remaining).

(4) Where you have completed linking the applications provided by the Company with one another, your in-app currency is also linked, and the information on what you have purchased or used in each application is reflected in all of the applications.

[How users can check their unused balance]
You can check the unused balance of each of your paid in-app currency and free in-app currency on the "My Page" screen of each application.

[App Terms of Service]
Please refer to the terms of service from within each application.

[Purport of the provisions of Article 14, Paragraph 1 of the Payment Services Act]
As a system for protecting holders of prepaid payment instruments, issuers are obliged under the Payment Services Act to secure funds by, among other measures, depositing with a Legal Affairs Bureau an issuance guarantee deposit in an amount equal to at least half of the unused balance of the prepaid payment instruments as of March 31 and September 30 of each year.

[Substance of the rights provided for in Article 31, Paragraph 1 of the Payment Services Act]
In the unlikely event that such a situation arises, holders of the prepaid payment instruments may, pursuant to the provisions of Article 31 of the Payment Services Act, receive payment from the issuance guarantee deposit secured in advance in priority to other creditors.

[Classification of the issuance guarantee deposit, issuance guarantee agreement, or issuance guarantee trust agreement]
The Company secures user funds by the method of depositing money.

[Policy on compensation and other responses for losses arising from unauthorized transactions]
If a third party uses the in-app currency held by a customer as a result of the loss or theft of the customer's device or the like, any damage incurred by the user in connection with such use shall be borne by the user.
