Purposes of use

Where account linking via a Google account or an Apple account is used, Astran Inc. (2-7-4 Aomi, Koto-ku, Tokyo; hereinafter "the Company") uses the user identifier, email address, name or display name, and similar information obtained from the external authentication service for the purposes of login authentication, user identification, storing, transferring and restoring learning data, streaks, characters, items, purchase information and the like, preventing unauthorized use, and managing and linking accounts that can be used in common across the apps and services provided by the Company.

Information obtained through Apple account linking

  • The user's email address

  • The user's private email address

  • The user's name as registered with their Apple account

Information obtained through Google account linking

  • The user's email address

  • The user's name as registered with their Google account

  • The user's profile image as registered with their Google account
