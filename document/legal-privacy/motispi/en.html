Astran Inc. (hereinafter referred to as "the Company") hereby establishes the Motispi Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as "this Policy") as set forth below with respect to the handling of information, including personal information, concerning customers who use "Motispi" (hereinafter referred to as "this App") provided by the Company (such customers hereinafter referred to as "the User"; such information hereinafter referred to as "User Information").

1. User Information Collected, Methods of Collection, and Purposes of Use

  1. In this Policy, "User Information" means personal information, information relating to the identification of the User, behavioral history on communication services, and other information generated or accumulated in relation to the User or the User's device, which the Company collects pursuant to this Policy.

  2. The Company uses the User Information described in each of the following items within the scope of the respective purposes of use set forth below (with respect to User Information that does not by itself constitute personal information, this includes not only User Information obtained directly from the User but also User Information obtained through provision by third parties such as advertising businesses).

Furthermore, where the purposes of use are separately specified to the User at the time User Information is obtained, such User Information will be used for the purposes specified at the time of collection.

• Information entered by the User upon registration for the service
Examples: user name, date of birth, prefecture of residence, high school of origin or current enrollment, email address, etc.

Where the User has linked an account with Apple, Google, or the like, for purposes such as device transfer and synchronization

To address acts that violate the terms of use of the service or other rules established by the Company, and otherwise to investigate, detect, and prevent improper use of the service

For the provision of personal data to third parties as set forth in this Policy

Information entered by the User when making an inquiry

To respond to inquiries from the User

  1. Provision of Personal Data to Third Parties

The Company may provide the personal data of Users who use this App to third parties as set forth below.

In addition, the Company may provide personal data to third parties where the User has given consent.


  • Advertising Distribution, etc.

[Purpose of Provision]

In order to deliver advertisements best suited to the User, to measure advertising effectiveness, and otherwise to optimize advertisements distributed by the Company or by third parties, the Company provides such information to advertising businesses.

[Items Provided]

Email address, device and app information, network information, information relating to access history, and other information necessary for advertising distribution, measurement of advertising effectiveness, and optimization of advertising

[Purpose of Provision]

[Items Provided]

Device and app information, information relating to access history, and other information necessary for linkage with services relating to the measurement of study records

3. Method of Suspending Use, etc.

If the User wishes to request the suspension of the collection of User Information by this App, please uninstall this App. In addition, if the User clicks the "Delete Account" button, the Company will cease the use of User Information relating to the User of that account. Please note that, because the collection or use of certain items of User Information is a prerequisite for this service, the Company will cease such collection or use only in the event that the account is deleted.

4. Method of Obtaining Consent

Consent is obtained by means of the method of consenting to this Policy.

5. Procedure for Amending the Privacy Policy

The Company will amend this Policy as necessary. However, in the event of an amendment to this Policy that requires the User's consent under applicable laws and regulations, the amended Policy shall apply only to those Users who have consented to the amendment by the method prescribed by the Company. When the Company amends this Policy, it will announce the effective date and content of the amended Policy by posting it on the Company's website or by other appropriate means, or will notify the User thereof.
