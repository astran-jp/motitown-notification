Astran Inc. (hereinafter referred to as "the Company") hereby establishes this Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as "this Policy") as set forth below with respect to the handling of the personal information and personal data of customers (hereinafter referred to as "the User") that the Company acquires in the course of providing applications, web services, and other services (hereinafter collectively referred to as the "Services").

In addition to this Policy, where a privacy policy is established for an individual Service (hereinafter referred to as an "Individual Policy"), such Individual Policy shall apply together with this Policy.

The terms used in this Policy shall have the meanings defined in the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (hereinafter referred to as the "APPI").

1. Compliance with Applicable Laws, Regulations, and Guidelines

The Company shall comply with the APPI and other applicable laws and regulations, the guidelines and other rules established by the Personal Information Protection Commission, and this Policy, and shall handle personal information lawfully and appropriately.

2. Categories of Information Collected by the Company and the Purposes of Use

Please refer to the Individual Policies.

3. Provision to Third Parties

The Company may provide personal information to third parties in the cases set forth in the Individual Policies, as well as in cases of provision pursuant to any of the items of Article 27, Paragraph 1 of the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (such as cases based on laws and regulations).

4. Contact Point for Inquiries and Related Matters

  • Responses to Requests
    With respect to the personal data acquired by the Company, the Company accepts requests from the individual concerned or their agent for the following responses:
    • Notification of the purposes of use
    • Disclosure
    • Disclosure of records of provision to third parties
    • Correction, addition, or deletion of the content
    • Suspension of use or erasure
    • Suspension of provision to third parties

  • Inquiries
    For opinions, questions, the filing of complaints, and other inquiries regarding the handling of user information, please contact the following contact point.
    Astran Inc., Personal Information Handling Desk
    E-mail: motitan@astran.jp

5. Security Control Measures for Personal Data

The Company shall take necessary and appropriate measures for the prevention of leakage, loss, or damage of the personal data it handles and for the other security control of personal data. When having employees or contractors handle personal data, the Company shall exercise necessary and appropriate supervision over such employees or such contractors so as to ensure the security control of the personal data concerned.

6. Procedures for Amending the Privacy Policy

The Company may amend this Policy as necessary. However, in the event of an amendment to this Policy that requires the consent of the User under laws and regulations, the amended Policy shall apply only to Users who have consented to the amendment by the method prescribed by the Company. When amending this Policy, the Company shall publicize, or notify the User of, the effective date and the content of the amended Policy by posting on the Company's website or by other appropriate means.
