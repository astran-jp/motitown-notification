Our Story

The History of Motitown

It all started at a small construction site with no name. Ever since a handful of people began building a "learning app that's way too fun," residents from all over the country have moved into this town. Here is the whole story of Motitown so far, as a timeline. The resident count is updated daily, and every new record is carved into this history.

Prologue

Why a "town"?

What we're building isn't a vocabulary app or a conversation app. It's a town. Because learning is all connected. Learn more words, and conversation gets more fun. One kind of learning helps the next. So we figured it all belongs in the same town.

And this town has friends in it. People to work hard alongside, while your own learning path is built just for you. "Together with everyone" and "learning that's yours alone." Motitown has both.

Learning in Motitown doesn't stop at vocabulary and conversation. This town will keep evolving, and every kind of learning will connect here. A town that keeps growing with the people who live in it. This is the story of that town so far.
