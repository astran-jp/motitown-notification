How to play

See it on screen.

STEP 1

the next word

Picture, sound, meaning and phrases on one card. At the end of a level, it continues to the next.

2
STEP 2

Swipe sideways
for details

Go deep only on the words you care about: examples, similar words, related words and word origins. Try swiping the screens below.

3

yours

Do more than look. Four buttons on the right make the word yours.

STEP 4

It reads the word, meaning and example aloud and moves on. When it reads an example, the card slides to the example page. Speed: 0.5x to 3x.

5
STEP 5

You get a score as soon as you finish. Compare your voice with the model and try again. Sounds you missed come with a tip (MotiTan Premium).

6
STEP 6

as you flip

You earn a little BP while you are flipping, using the buttons, or auto-flipping. Tap < to finish and see the words you met.

Tips

What good players do.

  • Check "Hide meanings", recall the meaning, then tap to see it
  • Viewing a card does not change its saved rating. Tap the check mark on the right to update it
  • Tap the tag at the top to switch course, level or word list
  • You can also open it from Play in a word list, to flip through only the words you saved
