Tips

What good players do.

  • Speak loudly and clearly, right to the end of the phrase
  • Use Pass to skip a hard phrase
  • A quiet place helps the mic hear you
  • Choose Shadowing on the start screen to hear a model before you read
  • No need to say the "~" in a phrase
