You can now choose whether to join the Citizen League!

2026/06/10 16:00

Hello! It's Motibear!

You can now choose whether or not to take part in the Citizen League!

​

If you'd rather learn at your own pace, turn off participation in Profile > Settings > Citizen League Settings.

​

When it's off, you'll be out of the league from the next week, and league info will no longer appear on Home or your Profile.

Want to join again? You can turn the setting back on anytime!

​

Learn in the style that suits you best!
