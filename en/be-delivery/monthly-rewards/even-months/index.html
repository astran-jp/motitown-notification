Witch Miyu

For one special day, Miyu has put on a tall pointed hat and a witch's outfit. Perched on an enormous broom, she lets her usual refined manner rest for a while. With the autumn breeze flowing through her hair, she enjoys a carefree stroll through the sky.

Roasted Sweet Potato MotiBear

Disguised as an enormous roasted sweet potato, MotiBear is savoring the flavors of fall—with another piping-hot sweet potato in paw to munch on! One bite of that gentle sweetness brings an instant smile. Autumn's generous appetite fills both belly and heart.

King Obakēnu

A sparkling crown and heart-shaped sunglasses are King Obakēnu's trademarks. Despite being a ghost, he has no interest in scaring anyone! This exceptionally cheerful party spirit loves music and fun, and gets every gathering going wherever he appears.

Werewolf Johnny

On Halloween night, Johnny transforms from his usual zombie self into a werewolf. Those furry ears and that tail are only a costume... right!? When the full moon rises, he certainly seems wilder than usual. Only Johnny knows whether the transformation is real!

Lacrosse MotiBear

Inspired by the sporting season, MotiBear has taken up lacrosse. There's still plenty left to learn, but with a big stick in paw, determination is never in short supply! Chasing the ball with total focus often ends in a tumble. The bandage on MotiBear's cheek is proof of all that hard work—a tiny badge of honor.

Excellence Award

Sunset Dragon

When the evening sky turns crimson, the Sunset Dragon appears from nowhere. Twilight red washes over white scales as blazing wings carry the dragon majestically across the autumn sky. Beautiful, capricious, and seen only during the fleeting moment when day meets night, this dragon watches over the season from above.
