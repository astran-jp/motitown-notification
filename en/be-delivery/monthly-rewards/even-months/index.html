Nutcracker Soldier Johnny

Johnny plays the nutcracker soldier in the winter show at MotiBeat. In the red tunic that stagehand Takumi tailored for him, he hams up the stiff toy-soldier march until the whole house roars. His salute, though, is as crisp as a real soldier's.

Reindeer Hat

A reindeer hat in fluffy caramel fabric, with little antlers and a red nose. Put it on and you might get picked to pull the sleigh.

Excellence Award

Reindeer MotiBear

MotiBear, snug inside a brown reindeer suit. He loves the hood's red nose and grand antlers, and the bell on his collar jingles with every step. A sway of the antlers and a little hop is his signal to set off, and he eagerly leads the way down snowy night roads.

Gingerbread Miyu

Miyu, in a gingerbread dress, helps build the candy house for Café MotiBoost's winter window. Her icing lines are as straight as if she used a ruler, and owner Karen laughs that she is far too serious. On days when the cookies bake just right, she can't help swaying happily.

Black Santa Cap

A black Santa cap with fluffy gray trim. Put it on and you'll want to quietly leave a present and slip away.

Excellence Award

Black Santa Claus MotiBear

After the children's gifts are delivered, MotiBear changes into a black coat for a second, late-night round as Santa. He plays it cool with his hands in his pockets, yet his heaviest sack is the one full of treats for the night watch and the lamp keepers. When thanked, he looks away.
