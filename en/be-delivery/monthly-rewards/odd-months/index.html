Lunar Fashion Sora

Sora dresses up for fall in a special moon-inspired outfit. A checked vest and crater-patterned pants are the highlights of the look. With a light, floaty step like walking on the moon, Sora heads into town in search of style, ready to show off a new outfit under tonight's starry sky.

Mushroom MotiBear

Wearing an oversized mushroom hat, MotiBear sets off to explore the woods in fall. His mushroom backpack is full of nuts and little treasures found along the way. He toddles along to the crunch of fallen leaves, then plays hide-and-seek with his woodland friends under his favorite tree.

Excellence Award

Shimoru

Shimoru is a little fairy of the first frost, born on a chilly November morning. A fluffy scarf and a ginkgo-leaf brooch are Shimoru's treasures. Shimoru gets cold easily and loves warm drinks. Strolling along leaf-covered paths with Iroha, Shimoru quietly brings the first hints of winter.

Shichi-Go-San Johnny

Johnny arrives for Shichi-Go-San, Japan's celebration of children's growth, wearing a haori jacket with splendid golden patterns. Holding his favorite chitose-ame candy, he looks a little proud of himself. Taking careful steps in unfamiliar zori sandals, he quietly wishes for many more happy, healthy days of playing with his friends.

Mont Blanc MotiBear

With swirls of chestnut cream and a plump chestnut on top, MotiBear is dressed as a Mont Blanc dessert. Wearing a checked shirt and a brown apron, he eagerly gets to work making fall treats. Friends follow the sweet aroma as he serves up something guaranteed to bring a smile.

Excellence Award

Iroha

Iroha is a cheerful little robot who brings the warmth of fall. Its ginkgo-leaf antenna senses the changing seasons, and the maple leaf on its chest glows softly. Iroha loves gathering fallen leaves and keeping chilly Shimoru warm, brightening everyone's November with a kind heart.
