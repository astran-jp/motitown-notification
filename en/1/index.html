You Can Now Opt Out of the League!
[Citizen League Settings Update]

2026/05/27 12:00

Hello! It's Motibear!
With this update, you can now choose whether to join the Citizen League! If the rankings stress you out, you can now learn at your own pace.

Easy to switch in "Citizen League Settings"

Just open Profile → Settings → "Citizen League Settings" and flip the switch! You can change it anytime, as often as you like.

▲ Turning the switch off means "not participating"

When it's off (not participating)…

Turn it off whenever you want a break from the league. Here's what changes 👇

  • Starting the next week, you won't be placed in a league.
  • The league badge next to your name disappears.
  • League info is no longer shown on your Profile or Home.
  • You won't see that week's league results or promotion and demotion notices either.

When you turn it back on (rejoining)…

Whenever you want to come back, just turn it on again!

  • You'll rejoin the league the next time you earn BP.
  • You'll be placed in a league at a similar level, based on your results from the last week you took part.

* You can switch this setting on and off at any time.

* While you're not participating, you can still learn and earn BP as usual.

* You can find past announcements in the mailbox at the top right of Motitown.
