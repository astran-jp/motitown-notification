Chinese and Korean Readings

We've added readings for Chinese and Korean!

* To show Chinese readings (pinyin)
With Profile > Settings > Language Settings > Chinese
selected, turn on
Profile > Settings > Learning Settings > Show Pinyin

* To show Korean readings
With Profile > Settings > Language Settings > Korean
selected, turn on
Profile > Settings > Learning Settings > Show Hangul Readings

Parental Controls Added

We've added parental controls to the Family Plan!

* To set up parental controls
Go to Profile > Settings > Family Plan
and tap "Manage as Child".
When the child taps "Approve" on their device, the button changes to "Manage Settings" and you can adjust each setting.
