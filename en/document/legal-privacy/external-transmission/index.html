External Transmission of User Information

Astran Inc. (hereinafter referred to as "the Company") may transmit User data externally in order to use the services provided by the third parties listed below. External services that use User data combine items of User data to infer such matters as the User's area of residence, place of work, interests and preferences, gender, and age group, and use this to display content and advertisements tailored to website visitors, or to analyze access and thereby improve the design and user experience of the website. For details of each service, the data items used, and the purposes of use, please refer to the website of the respective service provider.
