The High School Motitan Cup Has Begun!

2025/06/02 14:00

Hello! It's Motibear! Nice work on Motitan today!

The High School Motitan Cup is now held every month!

The rewards have changed too, so check the details below!

Q. Is it free to take part?

A. Yes, taking part is free.

Q. When does the campaign run?

A. The qualifiers run from the 1st to the 25th of each month, and the finals run from the 26th to the end of the month.

Q. My points are not being added to my school's points. Why?

A. Please check on My Page in Motitan that the correct school name is registered. Also, once you reach the daily cap on points added to your school (2,000 BP), no more points are added that day. Note that points for the Solo Fighter Award have no daily cap.

Q. My school is not on the list. How can I register it?

A. Please check that the school name is typed correctly. If it still does not appear, tell us your school's name through the feedback form below and we will add it to the list as soon as possible.

                                 Feedback form

Q. I registered the wrong school by mistake. Can I change it?

A. You cannot change your school during the campaign period.

Q. Do the BP earned in the qualifiers carry over to the finals?

A. No, they do not. In the finals, every school starts from 0 BP.

Q. I'm not good at English. Do I still have a chance?

A. Yes, you do. In Motitan, your points reflect how much you study, so by studying a lot you can aim for the top of the Motitan Cup.

Q. How can we make a comeback?

A. Get as many schoolmates as you can to join. There is a limit on the BP one person can earn for the school per day, so the more members, the better. We also hold BP Boost events from time to time, during which the BP cap changes. Earning lots of BP during an event gives you a chance to turn things around. Boost event info is also posted on our official X account, so please follow us.

* You can find past announcements in the mailbox at the top right of Motitown.
