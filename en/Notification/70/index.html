52 Vocabulary Books, Free on the Web! 📚

2025/07/02 12:00

Hello! It's Motibear! Today I released something I poured my heart into: the "Motitan Vocabulary Books". They're not just ordinary vocabulary books.

These books hold everything I believe about education. Today, let me tell you about that belief and how the Motitan Vocabulary Books came to be.

I believe the value that education creates should be shared by all of humanity.

Education is what lets us take the knowledge of those before us for granted and build new knowledge on top of it.

That's why I think every single person's desire to learn is precious and deserves to be treasured without limit.

The Analects put it this way:

"Is it not a joy to learn together with friends who share your aspirations, inspiring one another?"

But in reality, wanting to learn isn't enough. Education comes with very real barriers of geography and money.

No money for cram school. No good school within reach. No idea how to study, and no way to find out. And it's hard to find friends who share your goals.

That precious desire to learn is being left behind.

I want to break down these walls with innovation. A world where anyone who wants to learn can always find companions and always find a way. A world where everyone can get the best education there is.

So I decided to start a revolution in the field of English vocabulary.

Until now, learning vocabulary meant buying a vocabulary book. But vocabulary books have clear limits.

A paper book doesn't know which words you already know. And when the world changes and the words worth learning change, it keeps showing you the same old words.

Worst of all, the lonely work of facing a page makes people believe that studying is boring.

That's why I built Motitan.

Motitan shows you the words you should learn right now, based on how you learn. Its content keeps being updated with the times.

And above all, having friends in the app makes learning fun. Learning and growing with friends is a joy.

One year after Motitan launched, many people now study happily in the world of Motitan, pushing each other to improve.

That makes me really happy, and it taught me something in return: friendly rivalry is fun at its core.

But after actually using Motitan, I also noticed that a vocabulary book has some advantages over the app.

The satisfaction of finishing a whole book. Seeing more words at a glance. The tangible feeling of "I've done up to this page."

How could we keep the strengths of a vocabulary book and deliver the best learning experience in the world?

The answer is the "Motitan Vocabulary Books" released today. They're special books that help you learn words until they become memories you can truly use.

I decided to reorganize all the word data in Motitan into vocabulary books and publish them on the web, for free.

And at a completely different level from the free word lists that have been on the web until now.

One look and you'll see these aren't ordinary vocabulary books.

Beyond meanings and example sentences, each word comes with an image, audio, phonetic symbols, related words, phrases, differences from synonyms, and English-English explanations, all reorganized so you can learn words until they become memories you can truly use.

Of course, the content keeps being updated as times change, so it's always the latest version.

There's also a feature to hide the Japanese or English while you memorize.

For those who really want to carry a paper book, we made them easy to print out and staple into a physical vocabulary book.

And since every book is also inside the Motitan app, you can review the words you learned on the web in Motitan's games or gather your weak words together.

Today, 52 of these books went live on the web, completely free. They cover a huge range: EIKEN, TOEIC, university and high school entrance exams, IELTS, TOEFL, elementary English, and English conversation.

If you're serious about learning English, please take a look.

And if you think they're good, share this learning opportunity with your friends. Learning alongside friends will surely make your own learning even more fun.

I'll keep building a world where everyone with the aspiration to learn can do so happily and effectively, in the way that suits them.

It's on our official X account too, so check it out.
