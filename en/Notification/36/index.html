[New ✨] Learn Words More Fun with My Wordbooks!

2025/04/09 19:00

Hello! It's Motibear! I've got big news today!

The long-awaited wordbook feature has arrived in Motitan!

What's great about wordbooks

• Create your own My Wordbook!

• Add any words you want to learn!

• Play Battles and Adventures with the words you've added!

• Pick out your weak words and review them efficiently!

It's super easy to use!

1. Create a My Wordbook from the "Create" button at the top right!

2. Search for and add any word from the "Add Words" button!

3. Play Battles and Adventures with the words in your My Wordbook!

You can freely set the number of words, the range, and the order.

4. Before and after regular Battles and Adventures, swipe to add any word that catches your eye!

5. Search freely across all words!

You can add words to your My Wordbook right from the search results.

My Wordbooks are super handy for school tests, tackling weak spots, or organizing by topic!

Coming in a future update🌟

In an upcoming update, "Motitan AI Picks" will analyze your memory and automatically list the best words for you. Stay tuned!

With your very own My Wordbook, stay motivated and have more fun learning words!
