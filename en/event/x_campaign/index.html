Motitan & Motispi Official Account

How to Join

  1. 1Follow the official Motitan & Motispi account
  2. 2Add your Motitan/Motispi ID to your X profile
    (e.g., Motitan ID: A12345)
  3. 3Complete the specified actions on X to earn entries!

Every day, we hold a drawing from all entries, and 20% of players receive a limited Gacha.

Actions to Earn Entries

Interact with posts from the official account to earn entries!

  • Post on X with #モチタン or #モチスピ1 entry
  • Like1 entry
  • Repost1 entry
  • Reply2 entries
  • Quote post2 entries

Likes, reposts, replies, and quote posts count once a day per post by the official Motitan & Motispi account, and only when done on the day the post was published.
The more entries you have, the better your chances of winning.

Notes

  • The more entries you earn, the better your chances of winning. (Example: with likes, replies, reposts, and quote posts, you can earn up to 7 entries per post.)
  • Likes, replies, reposts, and quote posts count only when done on the same day the official Motitan & Motispi account posted.
  • Only actions on the official Motitan & Motispi account's own posts count. Actions on its replies do not.
  • Every day, we hold a drawing from all eligible entries, and 20% of them win. (Example: if there are 1,000 entries, 200 entries win.)
  • You can win up to once a day.
  • Each day's count runs from 4:00 AM to 3:59 AM the next day.
  • Winners are announced by receiving the prize Gacha in the app, from the next business day up to 5 business days later.
  • You won't get duplicate characters from the limited Gacha or the Last Five Prize.
  • If you win after collecting every character from the limited Gacha and the Last Five Prize, you'll receive 1 Gem.
